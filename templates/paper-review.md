# Paper Review

- **Review mode:** conference / journal / methodology / statistical / reproducibility / internal-adversarial
- **Scope and materials reviewed:**
- **Summary of contribution (neutral):**
- **Overall assessment and confidence:**

## Fatal Issues

For each: issue, evidence/location, impact on conclusions, and what would resolve it.

## Major Issues

For each: issue, evidence/location, impact, and actionable request.

## Minor Issues

For each: issue, evidence/location, and concise suggestion.

## Optional Suggestions

For each: potential benefit and why optional.

## Strengths

Tie each strength to a specific contribution or evidence location.

## Limitations / Questions

Separate missing information, uncertainty, and reviewer preference. Do not reject a method solely because another method is preferred.

Use the [review rubric](../shared/review-rubric.md) and [evidence policy](../shared/evidence-policy.md) for consistent assessment.
