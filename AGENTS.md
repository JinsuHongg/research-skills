# Contributor Instructions

Apply these rules to every change in this public repository.

## Public Safety

- Keep content public-safe and project-agnostic. Do not add personal, institutional, credential, unpublished-project, user-specific, proprietary, private-data, or confidential-review information.
- Do not put real private context into templates, examples, documentation, comments, or commit messages. Keep private project context outside this repository.
- Use synthetic, toy, or fully public benchmark examples only. Identify public-source provenance and applicable terms; label illustrative numbers as synthetic.

## Research Integrity

- Never invent academic references, source details, experimental results, theorem statements, statistical significance, or venue requirements.
- Preserve the distinction between source-backed facts, observations, interpretation, hypotheses, and unsupported claims.
- Mark unknown and unverified information explicitly. Prefer primary sources and verify semantic support.
- Treat venue deadlines, page limits, tracks, anonymity, and submission rules as changing. Require current official-source verification.

## Structure and Maintenance

- Keep each skill modular, with one clear responsibility and the common operational sections used by neighboring skills.
- Keep skill instructions concise and actionable. Put shared policies in `shared/` and link to them instead of copying long text.
- Use blank reusable artifacts in `templates/`; do not fill them with plausible-looking sample findings.
- Validate relative Markdown links and referenced paths after moving or renaming files.
- Update `README.md` when adding or removing a major skill or shared resource.
- Do not add elaborate software infrastructure unless a separate approved proposal establishes its need.
