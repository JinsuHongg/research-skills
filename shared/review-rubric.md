# Research Paper Review Rubric

Assess each area with evidence and locations. Use **not applicable** only with a reason; distinguish missing information from an established flaw.

| Area | Review questions |
|---|---|
| Problem and importance | Is the problem clear, relevant, and scoped? |
| Novelty and context | Is the closest prior work identified and compared fairly? |
| Technical correctness | Do definitions, derivations, algorithms, and assumptions support the method? |
| Methodology | Can the method answer the stated questions under its assumptions? |
| Experimental design | Are splits, baselines, metrics, tuning, and ablations appropriate and fair? |
| Statistical validity | Are variability, effect sizes, tests, and limitations reported appropriately? |
| Evidence and claims | Does each major claim match the available evidence and its scope? |
| Reproducibility | Are data, procedures, settings, and artifacts sufficiently described? |
| Clarity | Can readers follow the method, results, and limitations? |
| Limitations and ethics | Are relevant limitations and risks acknowledged? |

Classify findings as **fatal**, **major**, **minor**, or **optional** and explain impact. Separate objective problems from preferences. See [evidence-policy.md](evidence-policy.md) and [reproducibility-checklist.md](reproducibility-checklist.md).
