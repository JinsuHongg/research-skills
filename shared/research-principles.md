# Research Principles

Use these principles throughout a research workflow:

1. **Trace claims to evidence.** Separate source-backed facts, direct observations, interpretations, and open questions.
2. **Preserve uncertainty.** Record what is unknown, ambiguous, or unverified; do not fill gaps with plausible details.
3. **Design to falsify.** State what result would weaken or reject each hypothesis, not only what would support it.
4. **Protect evaluation integrity.** Keep test data out of training, tuning, calibration, and model selection unless the protocol explicitly permits a use and reports it.
5. **Compare fairly.** State baseline selection and tuning budgets; apply comparable data, compute, and selection procedures.
6. **Report limits and failures.** Include negative, variable, and failed outcomes that affect interpretation.
7. **Keep project context private.** Public examples and shared artifacts must not contain personal, institutional, unpublished, proprietary, or confidential material.
8. **Verify changing facts.** Check current official sources for venue or tool requirements before relying on them.

For evidence categories and claim strength, use [evidence-policy.md](evidence-policy.md). For source handling, use [citation-policy.md](citation-policy.md).
