# Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS)

**Scope orientation:** Interdisciplinary research around machine learning and neural information processing, with adjacent areas and tracks that may vary by year.

**Official starting point:** [About NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/About)

Before submission, verify the current official call for papers, formatting rules, page limits, anonymity policy, supplementary-material rules, and official LaTeX template for the relevant year and track. NeurIPS tracks can have distinct requirements; this profile is not a compliance checklist.
