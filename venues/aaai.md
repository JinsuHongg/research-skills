# AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI)

**Scope orientation:** Broad theoretical and applied artificial intelligence research, including work across AI subfields and interdisciplinary areas.

**Official starting point:** [AAAI Conference](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/)

Before submission, verify the current official call for papers, formatting rules, page limits, anonymity policy, supplementary-material rules, and official LaTeX template for the relevant year and track. Do not reuse requirements from another AAAI track or year without checking.
